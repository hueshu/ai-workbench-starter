# Rules

## Always stop and put it in the inbox
- Spending money, payments, subscriptions
- Sending anything out: messages, email, publishing, submitting
- Deleting, or overwriting originals
- Logins, passwords, verification codes
- My own additions: none

## Everything else
The AI decides and does it, then records it in log.md.

## Whoever did the work cannot call it done
Write "awaiting check" and have a new window look at the evidence. No evidence means not done.

## Before asking me, check
- Whether I already answered it under "Handled" in inbox.md. If so, follow that.
- Tentative wording is not a decision. Reply with one yes-or-no question.

## Never write into these files
Passwords, keys, ID numbers, bank cards, customers' private data.
